# /instagram-products

Turn your niche expertise into a complete Instagram sales machine. This skill runs through 7 proven prompt templates that cover every stage — from product idea to DM conversion — without sounding salesy.

## Usage

```
/instagram-products                    # Full interactive session, guides through all 7 steps
/instagram-products idea               # Step 1 only: Generate 5 digital product ideas
/instagram-products outline            # Step 2 only: Build a complete product outline
/instagram-products bio                # Step 3 only: Write 5 Instagram bios
/instagram-products carousel           # Step 4 only: Create a 9-slide carousel
/instagram-products caption            # Step 5 only: Write a sales caption
/instagram-products reel               # Step 6 only: Write a 60-second Reel script
/instagram-products dm                 # Step 7 only: Write a 3-message DM sequence
```

## What You Must Do When Invoked

### Gather Context First

Before running any step, collect this information from the user if not already provided:

- **Niche [X]** — e.g. "mindset coaching", "watercolor art", "freelance copywriting"
- **Skills & experience [Y]** — what they're good at
- **Target audience [Z]** — who they serve
- **Main problem [problem]** — what keeps their audience stuck
- **Product name [product name]** — if they already have one (for steps 3–7)
- **Transformation [transformation/result]** — the outcome their product delivers
- **Price point [price point]** — for the outline
- **Keyword [keyword]** — for CTAs (e.g. "FREE GUIDE", "TEMPLATES")

If running the full session (`/instagram-products` with no argument), collect all of the above before proceeding. Ask in one message, not question by question.

---

### Step 1 — The Digital Product Idea Generator

**Prompt to execute:**

> "I want to create a digital product to sell on Instagram. My niche is [X]. My skills and experience are [Y]. My target audience is [Z] and their biggest problem is [problem]. Give me the top 5 most profitable digital product ideas for this audience, ranked by ease of creation and income potential. For each one tell me: what to name it, what to include, what price point to sell it at and why, and which format works best (ebook, template, course, guide, checklist). Be specific. No generic advice."

Output all 5 ideas in a clean table: Name | Format | Price | Why it sells | What's inside.

---

### Step 2 — The Product Outline Builder

**Prompt to execute:**

> "I want to create a [type of digital product] about [topic] for [target audience]. Build me a complete outline with: (1) a compelling product name that sells, (2) a subtitle that clearly explains the transformation, (3) a full table of contents with 5-7 chapters or sections, (4) 3-5 bullet points of what the buyer will learn in each section, (5) a quick win they get in the first 10 minutes of using it. Make it feel premium and worth [price point]."

---

### Step 3 — The Instagram Bio Converter

**Prompt to execute:**

> "Write me 5 Instagram bio options that sell my digital product without being pushy. My product is [product name]. It helps [target audience] to [transformation/result]. My name is [name]. Include: who I help, what result they get, and a clear CTA to click the link in bio. Each bio must be under 150 characters. Make them sound human, warm, and authentic. Not corporate. Not salesy."

Present all 5 as copy-paste ready options, each on its own line.

---

### Step 4 — The Carousel Content Machine

**Prompt to execute:**

> "Create a 9-slide Instagram carousel that sells my digital product without feeling like an ad. My product is [product name] and it helps [audience] achieve [result]. Slide 1: a viral hook using a real news event or celebrity quote. Slides 2-6: pure value teaching one thing my product solves. Slide 7: a myth about this topic that my product disproves. Slide 8: social proof or transformation story. Slide 9: soft CTA to comment [keyword] for my free training. Tone: warm, real, conversational. No corporate language."

Format as Slide 1:, Slide 2:, etc. with the full text for each slide.

---

### Step 5 — The Sales Caption Writer

**Prompt to execute:**

> "Write an Instagram caption that sells my digital product [product name] without sounding salesy. Start with a one-line hook that stops the scroll. Then tell a short personal story about why I created this product. Then list 3 things the buyer will be able to do after purchasing. Then add one line of social proof. End with a soft CTA to comment [keyword]. Keep it under 300 words. Tone: genuine, big sister energy, warm and encouraging. No buzzwords. No hype."

---

### Step 6 — The Story Selling Script

**Prompt to execute:**

> "Write me a 60-second Instagram Reel script that sells my digital product [product name] through storytelling. Structure it like this: (1) open with a relatable problem my audience faces every day, (2) introduce myself and my turning point moment, (3) reveal the solution I discovered, (4) share one specific result or transformation, (5) end with a CTA to comment [keyword]. No hard selling. No fake hype. Write it the way a real person talks, not how a marketer pitches. Include text-on-screen suggestions for each part."

---

### Step 7 — The DM Automation Sequence

**Prompt to execute:**

> "Write a 3-message Instagram DM sequence for someone who commented [keyword] on my post about [topic]. I sell a digital product called [product name] that helps [audience] achieve [result]. Message 1: deliver the freebie or training warmly and make them feel seen. Message 2: send 24 hours later, share a transformation story of someone who used my product and got [result]. Message 3: send 48 hours later, invite them to purchase with a soft urgency line. Tone: warm, genuine, like a friend who wants to help. No pressure. No pushy sales language."

## Important Tone Rules (Apply to All Steps)

- Warm, genuine, conversational — never corporate
- No buzzwords: "game-changer", "skyrocket", "crush it"
- No fake hype or urgency
- Write like a real person talking to a friend
- The product, story, and voice belong to the user — Claude provides the structure
